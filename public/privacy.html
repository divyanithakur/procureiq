Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

ProcureIQ respects the privacy of people who use our website and product. This policy explains the categories of information we may collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect account information, contact details, authentication information, product usage information, and information you choose to submit through forms or the ProcureIQ workspace. Files uploaded for procurement analysis may contain business information, so users should only upload information they are authorized to provide.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the service, process requested analyses, respond to support or sales enquiries, maintain product functionality, and improve reliability. ProcureIQ does not need or intentionally store your account password in its procurement database.

AI-assisted analysis

When you request an AI insight, ProcureIQ may send the minimum procurement facts needed for that request, such as material, supplier, price, quantity, and calculated comparison values, to the configured AI provider. Authentication credentials, payment secrets, and database credentials are not sent as part of AI prompts. AI output is treated as decision support and should be checked against your source records.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

We may use service providers that help operate authentication, hosting, payments, AI-assisted analysis, communications, or other product infrastructure. Each provider may process the information necessary for the service it provides. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of the service and its users.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and legitimate business records. You may contact us to request information about your data or applicable deletion options.
