Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Subscriptions

Paid ProcureIQ plans are recurring monthly subscriptions. A successful subscription activates the selected plan for the current billing period and renews automatically until cancelled.

Cancellation

You can cancel an active subscription from Plans & Billing. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period, so access remains available for the period already paid for and the next renewal is not charged.

Refunds

Cancellation stops future renewals but does not by itself reverse a captured payment. If you believe a payment should be refunded, contact ProcureIQ with the account and payment details needed to locate the transaction. Refund eligibility is reviewed against the circumstances of the request and, when approved, is returned through the original payment method where supported.

Failed payments

A declined or failed payment does not create a successful paid entitlement. Razorpay may retry eligible recurring payments according to its subscription rules. If a renewal remains unpaid, paid access may be suspended when the subscription status is no longer active.
